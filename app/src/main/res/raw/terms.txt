# Terms

Torrents is a free BitTorrent client for the Mudita Kompakt, a fork of LibreTorrent by Yaroslav
Pronin, made by wander wildwood. Contact: email@wanderthe.dev

## What it does

It downloads and shares files directly with other people over the BitTorrent network. It does
not host, index, search for or recommend any content, and it supplies none.
